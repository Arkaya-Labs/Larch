## Plan by day

**Friday 23 October.** Arrive at 07:05, take the metro to Baixa-Chiado, leave bags at the hotel. Walk the Alfama and have lunch at the tasca on Rua dos Remedios. Early night.

**Saturday 24 October.** Tram 28 in the morning before the crowds, Sao Jorge castle, sunset at the Miradouro da Graca. Dinner booked at Casa Lume, 20:30.

**Sunday 25 October.** Day trip to Sintra: train from Rossio station at 09:11. Pena Palace tickets are for the 11:00 slot. Back by 18:00.

**Monday 26 October.** Belem: the monastery, the tower and custard tarts at the old bakery. Afternoon at the tile museum.

**Tuesday 27 October.** Free day. Maybe the beach at Cascais if the weather holds, otherwise the Gulbenkian museum and garden.

**Wednesday 28 October.** Food tour at 11:00, meeting point Praca da Figueira. Pack in the evening.
