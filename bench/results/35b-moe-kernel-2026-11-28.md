- **Z, expert adapters:** nothing in a load's options says an expert adapter will be used: adapters attach after the
  load, through `EngineHandle::set_router_deltas` (the library's `set_router_deltas`), at a step boundary with no
  started request. So nothing changed. Router deltas alone (rank 0) still apply on a split load; an adapter (rank > 0)
  is refused with `larch_expert_cache_no_learning`. What a reload on attach would take: on that refusal the engine
  queues a reload whose plan keeps whole layers (a per-load "no split" the loader reads beside its seats, since
  `LARCH_EXPERT_CACHE` is read once at start), drains, loads (about 4 to 5 s on m13 warm, plus the whole-layer
  plan's lower decode: A's 237.7 against 289.8 tok/s at W4@4 last night), then applies the deltas; the version change
  already drops the KV and prefix cache. Not built.
- **S, the many-chats setting:** no behaviour change was needed. `the_many_chats_setting_plans_eight_slots_once` (fake
  CUDA, the shipped-profile path): 4 and 8 seats each plan their split once at 4 and 8 slots; `replan_due` re-plans a
  live 8 on a 4-slot split and a live 4 on an 8-slot one; demand never asks 4 slots for more. On m13, `--set
  max_batch_seqs=4` plans the default's exact split (`larch_expert_cache_plan,20,9319,…,823610543`, the same reserve),

## K: the kernel

`pair_dot<T, G>` (kernels/src/cuda/quant.cu): a group of G lanes a column; lane gl sums units gl + G j of `unit<T>` in j
order, the next unit's loads in flight while one decodes (as `mmvq_k`), `fast<T>`'s decode and `mmvq_col`'s FMA order,
then the group's butterfly. G is 16 where a row has under 32 units (m13's down, k 512: two columns a warp), else 32.
`mmvq_id_k` (hot, and cold through `mmvq_id_mixed`'s table), `moe_gate_up_k` and `moe_down_k` call it through `id_pair`
on CUDA for every dtype with a `fast<T>` decoder; `id_dot` stays for Q2_K and HIP (warpSize 64, `mmvq_id_k` as before).
Q3_K's 16-byte chunks come in as the two aligned 16-byte words around them (`ld16_win`: two requests for eight 2-byte
loads, reads ending inside the block), and on a cold row read over the bus its scales and d in one more window (not in
a row's last block). Steps, each measured (fused_bench and g1, the results' `k-hot.txt` and `k-cold.txt` are the last):

| step | gate_up + down a layer, 1 / 4 / 8 rows (us) | cold pair, 1 / 4 / 8 rows (us) |
|---|---|---|
| A (id_dot) | 36.51 / 115.28 / 221.68 | 364.0 / 369.2 / 304.8 |
| unit loads, next in flight, G 16 for down | 28.36 / 90.17 / 174.32 | 257.6 / 262.7 / 209.7 |
| + an L2 prefetch of each cold row | (not run) | 265.0 / 296.0 / 250.5: slower, reverted |
| + the widest load each Q3_K chunk's address allows | 30.18 / 93.04 / 179.25 | 299.0 / 305.3 / 249.9: slower (divergence), reverted |
| + ld16_win for Q3_K chunks | 27.30 / 85.23 / 163.63 | 207.3 / 255.7 / 201.8 |
| + Q3_K scales by ld16_win on every row | 28.26 / 88.33 / 171.93 | 183.8 / 212.0 / 163.5: the hot decode 7 % slower |
| **kept: scales by ld16_win on cold rows only** | **27.26 / 84.24 / 162.81** | **189.7 / 203.6 / 161.7** |

The 4-row pair is 26.9 % faster and the 1- and 8-row ones 25.3 % and 26.6 % faster; the cold pair 48 %, 45 % and 47 %
cheaper. G-cold's 180 µs is not reached at 1 and 4 rows (189.7, 203.6): the next lever is the cold Q4_K down (44 µs for
590 KB, 13 GB/s), whose weighted sum runs the slots one after another.

## Method

- Every GPU job in one `flock` hold; before each, no build, `tmux ls` (one session), used VRAM under 2.5 GB (313 MiB),
  the core under 85 C. Builds under `nice -n 19`; no build, test or review during a timed run.
- Serve runs: `larch bench -m m13.gguf --mode serve --concurrency <c> --set max_context=2560 --machine wsl5070
  [--slice 1/10] [--set max_batch_seqs=8]` with `LARCH_FIT_DIAGNOSTICS=1 LARCH_PROFILE_STEPS=1
  LARCH_GRAPH_DIAGNOSTICS=1`; A B B A noise rule: under 2 % or under either side's spread.
- fused_bench: `nvcc -O3 -std=c++20 -arch=sm_120 -ccbin g++-14 -Ikernels/include kernels/tests/fused_bench.cu
  <build>/liblarch_kernels.a -lcublas`, `--rows 1,4,8`. g1: the same flags on the results' `g1.cu`.
- Parity: `larch parity --device 0 -m m8.gguf --ref <m8 cuBLAS dump> --bound 0.021716577412409198 --margin
  0.42968761920928955 --mode decode|chunked`; `larch parity -m m13.gguf --ref <35b cuBLAS dump> --bound
  0.035366004871120384 --margin 3.3277282305061817 --mode all`.
- llama.cpp: `llama-serve-bench run --workload chat.jsonl --concurrency 8 -m m13.gguf -ngl 99 -fa on -ctk f16 -ctv f16
  -t 8 -b 2048 -ub 1024 -c 20480 --n-cpu-moe 3`.
