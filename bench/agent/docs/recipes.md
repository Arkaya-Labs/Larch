# Family Recipe Collection

Recipes collected from Grandma's notebook, friends and a few websites, typed up in 2025.
Oven temperatures are for a conventional oven; take 20 degrees off for a fan oven.

## Grandma's banana bread

Makes one loaf.

Ingredients:
- 3 very ripe bananas
- 80 g melted butter
- 150 g brown sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 tsp baking soda
- a pinch of salt
- 190 g plain flour
- a handful of chopped walnuts

Method:
1. Heat the oven and grease a 23 x 13 cm loaf tin.
2. Mash the bananas in a bowl and stir in the butter.
3. Mix in the sugar, egg and vanilla, then the baking soda and salt.
4. Fold in the flour and walnuts until just combined.
5. Bake at 175 °C for 60 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
6. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then on a rack.

Note: Freezes well in slices. Grandma used dates when there were no walnuts.

## Red lentil soup

Serves 6.

Ingredients:
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp cumin
- 250 g red lentils
- 1.5 litres vegetable stock
- 1 lemon

Method:
1. Soften the onion and carrots in the oil for 8 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and cumin for one minute.
3. Add the lentils and stock, bring to the boil and simmer for 25 minutes.
4. Blend half of the soup and stir it back in.
5. Season and finish with lemon juice.

Note: Thickens overnight; add water when reheating.

## Weeknight chicken traybake

Serves 4.

Ingredients:
- 8 chicken thighs
- 600 g small potatoes, halved
- 2 red onions, in wedges
- 1 lemon, quartered
- 4 sprigs rosemary
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- salt and pepper

Method:
1. Toss everything in a large roasting tin.
2. Roast at 200 °C for 45 minutes, turning once.
3. Rest for 5 minutes and squeeze over the roasted lemon.

Note: Swap the potatoes for squash in autumn.

## Overnight oats

Serves 2.

Ingredients:
- 100 g rolled oats
- 250 ml milk
- 100 g yoghurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tbsp honey
- berries to serve

Method:
1. Stir the oats, milk, yoghurt, chia and honey together.
2. Cover and chill overnight.
3. Top with berries in the morning.

Note: Keeps for two days in the fridge.

## Mushroom risotto

Serves 4.

Ingredients:
- 30 g dried porcini
- 1 litre hot stock
- 1 onion
- 300 g arborio rice
- 125 ml white wine
- 250 g chestnut mushrooms
- 50 g parmesan
- 30 g butter

Method:
1. Soak the porcini in 250 ml hot water for 20 minutes.
2. Soften the onion, add the rice and toast it.
3. Add the wine, then the stock a ladle at a time for 18 minutes, stirring.
4. Fry the fresh mushrooms and stir them in with the porcini and its liquid.
5. Beat in the butter and parmesan and rest for 2 minutes.

Note: Leftovers make good arancini.

## Black bean chilli

Serves 6.

Ingredients:
- 2 onions
- 2 peppers
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp chipotle
- 2 tins black beans
- 2 tins chopped tomatoes
- 1 square dark chocolate

Method:
1. Fry the onions and peppers until soft.
2. Add the garlic and spices for a minute.
3. Add the beans and tomatoes and simmer for 40 minutes.
4. Stir in the chocolate and season.

Note: Better on the second day. Serve with rice or tortilla chips.

## Lemon drizzle cake

Makes one 20 cm cake.

Ingredients:
- 225 g soft butter
- 225 g caster sugar
- 4 eggs
- 225 g self-raising flour
- 2 lemons
- 85 g sugar for the drizzle

Method:
1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale.
2. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour and zest.
3. Bake at 180 °C for 45 minutes.
4. Mix the lemon juice with the drizzle sugar and pour over the warm cake.

Note: Prick the cake all over before the drizzle so it soaks in.

## Shakshuka

Serves 2.

Ingredients:
- 1 onion
- 1 red pepper
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tin tomatoes
- 4 eggs
- feta and parsley

Method:
1. Soften the onion and pepper.
2. Add the garlic and spices, then the tomatoes; simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Make four wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook for 6 minutes.
4. Finish with feta and parsley.

Note: Serve with crusty bread.

## Pancakes

Makes 12.

Ingredients:
- 200 g plain flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp sugar
- a pinch of salt
- 300 ml milk
- 1 egg
- 25 g melted butter

Method:
1. Whisk the dry ingredients.
2. Whisk in the milk, egg and butter until smooth.
3. Cook ladles of batter in a hot pan until bubbles form, then flip.

Note: Sunday breakfast. Keep warm in a low oven.

## Roast vegetable lasagne

Serves 6.

Ingredients:
- 2 courgettes
- 1 aubergine
- 2 peppers
- 500 g passata
- 12 lasagne sheets
- 500 g ricotta
- 125 g mozzarella
- basil

Method:
1. Roast the chopped vegetables for 30 minutes.
2. Stir the vegetables into the passata.
3. Layer sauce, pasta and ricotta three times; top with mozzarella.
4. Bake at 190 °C for 40 minutes and rest for 10 minutes.

Note: Assemble the day before and keep it in the fridge.

## Thai green curry

Serves 4.

Ingredients:
- 2 tbsp green curry paste
- 400 ml coconut milk
- 500 g chicken or tofu
- 1 aubergine
- a handful of green beans
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 lime
- Thai basil

Method:
1. Fry the paste for one minute.
2. Add the coconut milk and bring to a simmer.
3. Add the chicken or tofu and vegetables and cook for 12 minutes.
4. Season with fish sauce and lime and add the basil.

Note: Use half the paste for the children.

## Apple crumble

Serves 6.

Ingredients:
- 6 cooking apples
- 50 g sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 150 g flour
- 100 g cold butter
- 75 g demerara sugar
- 50 g oats

Method:
1. Slice the apples into a dish with the sugar and cinnamon.
2. Rub the butter into the flour, then stir in the demerara and oats.
3. Scatter over the apples and bake at 180 °C for 35 minutes.

Note: Custard, not cream.

## Pesto pasta salad

Serves 4.

Ingredients:
- 300 g fusilli
- 4 tbsp pesto
- 200 g cherry tomatoes
- a handful of rocket
- 50 g pine nuts
- 1 ball mozzarella

Method:
1. Cook the pasta and cool it under cold water.
2. Toast the pine nuts in a dry pan.
3. Toss everything together and season.

Note: Picnics and packed lunches.

## Beef stew

Serves 6.

Ingredients:
- 1 kg stewing beef
- 2 tbsp flour
- 2 onions
- 3 carrots
- 2 sticks celery
- 500 ml stock
- 250 ml red wine
- 2 bay leaves

Method:
1. Toss the beef in the flour and brown it in batches.
2. Soften the vegetables in the same pot.
3. Add the beef, stock, wine and bay leaves.
4. Cover and cook in the oven at 160 °C for 3 hours.

Note: Add dumplings for the last 25 minutes.

## Granola

Makes one large jar.

Ingredients:
- 300 g oats
- 100 g mixed nuts
- 50 g seeds
- 4 tbsp maple syrup
- 3 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 100 g dried fruit

Method:
1. Mix everything except the fruit.
2. Spread on two trays and bake at 150 °C for 25 minutes, stirring twice.
3. Cool and stir in the fruit.

Note: Keeps for a month in an airtight jar.

## Carrot cake

Makes one 20 cm cake.

Ingredients:
- 200 g grated carrot
- 175 g brown sugar
- 3 eggs
- 150 ml oil
- 200 g self-raising flour
- 1 tsp mixed spice
- 100 g raisins
- cream cheese icing

Method:
1. Whisk the sugar, eggs and oil.
2. Fold in the flour, spice, carrot and raisins.
3. Bake at 170 °C for 50 minutes.
4. Ice when completely cold.

Note: Sam's birthday cake every year.

## Salad dressings

Shake everything in a jar. Each makes enough for a salad for four and keeps for a week in the fridge.

- House vinaigrette: 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, a pinch of sugar.
- Lemon and herb: 4 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1 lemon, a handful of chopped parsley and dill.
- Honey mustard: 3 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp cider vinegar, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard.
- Tahini: 2 tbsp tahini, juice of half a lemon, 1 small garlic clove, water to thin.
- Soy and ginger: 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sesame oil, grated ginger.
- Yoghurt and garlic: 100 g yoghurt, 1 crushed garlic clove, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt.
- Balsamic: 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar, a pinch of salt.
- Green goddess: 100 g yoghurt, 1 avocado, a bunch of basil and chives, lemon juice.

### Chicken stock

Bones from a roast chicken, 1 onion, 1 carrot, 1 celery stick, a bay leaf, peppercorns.

Cover with 2 litres of cold water, bring to a simmer, skim, and simmer gently for 3 hours. Strain and cool quickly.

Keeps: 4 days in the fridge, 3 months frozen.

### Vegetable stock

2 onions, 2 carrots, 2 celery sticks, mushroom stalks, parsley stalks, a bay leaf.

Cover with 2 litres of water and simmer for 45 minutes. Strain, pressing the vegetables.

Keeps: 4 days in the fridge, 3 months frozen.

### Basic tomato sauce

2 tins tomatoes, 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 garlic cloves, a pinch of sugar, basil.

Warm the sliced garlic in the oil, add the tomatoes and sugar, and simmer for 25 minutes. Add the basil at the end.

Keeps: 5 days in the fridge, 3 months frozen.

### White sauce

40 g butter, 40 g plain flour, 500 ml milk, nutmeg.

Melt the butter, stir in the flour for a minute, then whisk in the milk a little at a time. Simmer for 5 minutes, stirring, and season with nutmeg.

Keeps: 2 days in the fridge.

### Gravy

Roasting juices, 1 tbsp flour, 400 ml stock, a splash of wine.

Pour off most of the fat, stir the flour into the juices in the tin, add the wine and stock and simmer, scraping the tin, for 5 minutes. Strain.

Keeps: 2 days in the fridge.

### Shortcrust pastry

250 g plain flour, 125 g cold butter, a pinch of salt, 3 to 4 tbsp cold water.

Rub the butter into the flour, add water until it comes together, and chill for 30 minutes before rolling.

Keeps: 2 days in the fridge, 2 months frozen.

### Pizza dough

500 g strong flour, 7 g dried yeast, 1 tsp salt, 325 ml warm water, 2 tbsp oil.

Mix, knead for 10 minutes, and leave to rise for an hour. Makes four thin pizzas; bake on the hottest oven setting for 8 to 10 minutes.

Keeps: 1 day in the fridge, 1 month frozen.

### Custard

500 ml milk, 4 egg yolks, 50 g sugar, 1 tbsp cornflour, vanilla.

Heat the milk. Whisk the yolks, sugar and cornflour, pour on the hot milk, return to the pan and stir over a low heat until thick.

Keeps: 2 days in the fridge.

## Menus for a crowd

Scale the recipes by the number of guests, but not the salt or the spices: add those to taste. Cook what you can the day before.

**Summer barbecue for 12.** Burgers and halloumi, three salads (pesto pasta, potato, green), corn on the cob, and lemon drizzle cake. Buy two bags of charcoal and ice.

**Christmas Eve for 8.** Chestnut and mushroom soup, then roast vegetable lasagne and a green salad with the house vinaigrette, then apple crumble and custard.

**Birthday tea for 10.** Sandwiches, sausage rolls, crudités with the yoghurt and garlic dip, carrot cake and a fruit platter.

**Curry night for 6.** Thai green curry, black bean chilli for anyone who does not eat meat, rice, flatbreads, and mango with lime.

**Brunch for 6.** Shakshuka in two pans, pancakes, granola with yoghurt and berries, coffee and fresh orange juice.

**Soup and bread lunch for 10.** Two soups (one blended, one chunky), a big loaf from the bakery, cheese, butter and pickles, and banana bread with tea. Keep the soups warm on the lowest heat and set out bowls, spoons and a ladle for each.

**Picnic for 4.** Pesto pasta salad, a quiche from the shortcrust pastry, cherry tomatoes, crisps, strawberries and granola bars. Pack the dressing separately.
