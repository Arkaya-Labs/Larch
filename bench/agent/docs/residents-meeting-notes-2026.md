# Alder Street Residents' Association: meeting notes, 2026

Meetings are held on the second Wednesday of each month at 19:00 in the community room of building A. Notes are taken by the secretary and approved at the next meeting.

## Meeting of 14 January 2026

Present: Mei Tan (chair), Oscar Ruiz (treasurer), Fatima Haddad (secretary), Jordan Avery, Liam O'Neill, Grace Mensah. Apologies from Nora Lind.

The notes of the previous meeting were approved without changes.

### Treasurer's report

Annual dues remain $600 for 2026, payable by 31 March. The operating account stands at $18,000. Spending this month was within the
budget for cleaning, gardening and electricity in the common areas. The treasurer will circulate
the full statement by email. Receipts for anything a volunteer bought for the association go to
the treasurer within a month, with the date and what it was for, so they can be paid back.

### Maintenance

The boiler in building B was serviced; the gutters on the north side need clearing. The caretaker's report was noted. Residents are reminded to report repairs through the
association's form rather than by message to board members.

### Events

Winter clean-up day set for 7 February. Volunteers are welcome; sign up on the lobby noticeboard.

### Correspondence

A letter from the city about the new recycling bins, which arrive in March. Letters to the association go in the post box in the lobby of building A or to
the association's email address; the secretary answers within two weeks.

### Garden

The garden committee will prune the fruit trees in the courtyard in February. Anyone who wants to help in the garden can come on the first Saturday of the month
from 10:00; tools and gloves are in the shed by the bicycle rack.

### Safety

The fire alarm test on 6 January passed in all three buildings. Report anything unsafe in the common areas to the caretaker the same day; for an
emergency call the emergency services first.

### Open floor

Residents asked for more lighting on the path to the bicycle shed. The chair thanked everyone for coming.

### Next meeting

The next meeting is on 11 February 2026 at 19:00 in the community room of building A. Send agenda
items to the secretary at least a week before.

### Action items

- Secretary to post these notes on the noticeboard within a week.
- Treasurer to send the monthly statement.
- Chair to follow up the open-floor items before the next meeting.
