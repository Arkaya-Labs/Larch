# Code of conduct

Larch follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the repository, in issues and discussions, and anywhere else you represent the project.

In short: be considerate, assume good faith, and criticise code and ideas, not people. Harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted.

## Reporting

Email [conduct@arkaya-labs.com](mailto:conduct@arkaya-labs.com) with what happened, where, and links if you have them.
Reports are read by the maintainers at Arkaya Labs and kept confidential. If a maintainer is
involved, say so, and they won't take part in handling the report.

Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or ban accounts from the project, following the
enforcement guidelines in the Covenant.
